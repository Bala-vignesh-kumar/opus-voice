Fineract sync timeouts, and the Piper voice swap
Aug 28, 2026·9:41 PM·14 min·26 turns
Summary

You were chasing a flaky sync between Nakshatra Residence and the Apache Fineract instance — client transactions were occasionally landing twice on retry. We traced it to the loan repayment endpoint not being idempotent under Fineract's own retry policy, not a bug in your code. Along the way you asked opus voice to switch its neural voice, so we moved Piper over to the hfc_female-medium checkpoint and confirmed Whisper's small model still transcribed cleanly at the new sample rate before wrapping up.

Decisions
  • Wrap the loan repayment call in an idempotency key stored against the Nakshatra Residence tenant, rather than patching retries in Fineract itself.
  • Keep Piper on en_US-hfc_female-medium — the lighter checkpoint didn't hold up on longer sentences.
  • Leave Whisper on the small model; the accuracy difference against medium wasn't worth the extra load time.
Action items
  • Add the idempotency key column to the repayments table and thread it through the Fineract client. to-do 1
  • Write a regression test that fires the same repayment twice and asserts a single ledger entry. to-do 2
  • Sanity-check the new Piper voice against the wake-phrase latency budget once the fix ships.
